What is MMBTU?

MMBTU stands for Million British Thermal Units (the "MM" comes from the Roman numeral M = 1,000, so MM = 1,000,000). It is the standard unit for natural gas pricing in commodity markets, LNG trading, and large commercial/industrial energy contracts.

1 MMBTU = 1,000,000 BTU = 10 Therms = 293.001 kWh ≈ 26.853 m³ of natural gas (at standard conditions for typical UK natural gas).

MMBTU to M3 Formula (Natural Gas)
m³ = MMBTU × 26.853
M3 to MMBTU Formula
MMBTU = m³ ÷ 26.853
