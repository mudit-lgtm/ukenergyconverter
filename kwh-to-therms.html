kWh to Therms Formula

The reverse conversion is straightforward — divide the kWh value by 29.3001 to get therms. This is the exact inverse of the therms-to-kWh formula and is a fixed, exact conversion (no variables required).

kWh to Therms Formula
Therms = kWh ÷ 29.3001

One kilowatt hour equals approximately 0.034121 therms. This is a very small fraction because a therm is a large unit of energy — one therm contains the same energy as 29.3 kWh.
