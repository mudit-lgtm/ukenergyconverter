kW to M3/hr Formula

To convert a gas appliance's power output (kW) to the required gas flow rate (m³/hr), you divide the power by the energy content of the gas (CV × VCF):

kW to M3/hr Formula
m³/hr = kW × 3.6 ÷ (Calorific Value × 1.02264)
M3/hr to kW Formula
kW = m³/hr × Calorific Value × 1.02264 ÷ 3.6
