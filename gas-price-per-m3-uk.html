How Much Does 1 m3 of Gas Cost in the UK?

At the current approximate rate of 7.42p per kWh, 1 m³ of gas (which equals approximately 11.18 kWh at the standard CV) costs about 83p per cubic metre (11.18 × 7.42p = 82.96p, + 5% VAT = ~87p). This figure varies based on your actual unit rate and calorific value.
