Tips to Reduce Your Gas Usage

🌡️ Turn down the thermostat
Reducing your heating by just 1°C can cut gas usage by up to 10% and save around £100/year.
🚿 Cut hot water usage
Shorter showers and a more efficient boiler can reduce hot water heating by 20–30%.
🏠 Improve insulation
Loft and cavity wall insulation can reduce heat loss dramatically — often the single highest-impact improvement.
🔧 Service your boiler
An annual boiler service keeps it running at peak efficiency and catches issues before they become expensive.
