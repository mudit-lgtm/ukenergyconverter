Imperial Gas Meter Conversion Formula

Imperial meters measure gas in cubic feet (ft³). Before applying the standard Ofgem formula, you must first convert ft³ to m³ using the conversion factor of 0.0283168 (1 ft³ = 0.0283168 m³, or equivalently, multiply by 2.83168 then divide by 100).

Cubic Feet to kWh Formula
kWh = ft³ × 0.028317 × Calorific Value × 1.02264 ÷ 3.6

For quick estimates: 1 cubic foot ≈ 0.317 kWh (at CV 39.5). So 100 ft³ ≈ 31.7 kWh.
