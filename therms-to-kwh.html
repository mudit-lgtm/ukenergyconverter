What is a Therm?

A therm is a unit of heat energy equal to 100,000 British Thermal Units (BTU). It is most commonly used in the United States for natural gas billing, though some older UK gas tariffs used therms before the metrication of the gas industry in the 1970s and 1980s.

One therm equals exactly 29.3001 kWh or approximately 29.3 kWh. This is a fixed conversion — unlike the UK's m³ to kWh conversion, there is no calorific value variable involved because the therm is already defined as a fixed amount of energy.

Therms to kWh Formula
kWh = Therms × 29.3001
