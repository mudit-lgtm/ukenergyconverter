How to Convert Gas M3 to kWh

Converting gas cubic metres to kilowatt hours requires the official Ofgem formula, which is mandated for all UK energy suppliers. The conversion accounts for three key factors: the volume of gas used (in m³), the calorific value (energy content of the gas), and a volume correction factor that adjusts for temperature and pressure variations.

The calorific value represents how much energy is contained in each cubic metre of gas. This varies slightly by region and season — your energy supplier prints your specific value on every gas bill. The standard UK figure used for estimates is 39.5 kWh/m³, though values typically range from 37.5 to 43.0 kWh/m³ across the country.

Ofgem Official Gas Conversion Formula
kWh = m³ used × Calorific Value × 1.02264 ÷ 3.6
💡 Quick Check: 1 m³ of gas at the UK average CV of 39.5 = 11.18 kWh. Multiply your m³ usage by 11.18 for a fast estimate (±1% of full Ofgem formula).

What is the Calorific Value?

The calorific value (CV) measures the heat energy contained in each cubic metre of natural gas. It is affected by the composition of the gas supply, which varies slightly by region, pipeline and season. Ofgem requires suppliers to use the actual measured CV for billing, not a fixed estimate. This is why your gas bill shows a specific CV — often something like 39.3 or 40.1 kWh/m³.

When you check your bill, look for a section labelled "Gas used" or "Consumption details." The CV will be listed there, typically to one decimal place. If you're estimating and don't have a bill to hand, 39.5 kWh/m³ is the accepted UK average and is used by most online calculators and energy comparison tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many kWh is 1 m3 of gas?
Using the UK standard calorific value of 39.5 kWh/m³ and the Ofgem volume correction factor of 1.02264: (1 × 39.5 × 1.02264) ÷ 3.6 = 11.18 kWh. This is the widely accepted UK figure, though your exact value will vary slightly based on your regional CV.
What is the Ofgem formula for gas m3 to kWh?
The official formula is: kWh = m³ × Calorific Value × 1.02264 ÷ 3.6. Where 1.02264 is the volume correction factor (accounts for temperature and pressure) and 3.6 is the conversion factor from MJ to kWh. This is mandated by Ofgem and used by all licensed UK gas suppliers.
Where do I find my calorific value?
Your calorific value is printed on every gas bill. Look in the "Gas used" or "Consumption details" section — you'll see something like "Calorific value: 39.3 kWh/m³". It's usually listed near the unit rate and meter reading details.
Why does my gas bill calculation differ slightly from this calculator?
Small differences can occur because: (1) your bill uses your specific regional CV rather than the 39.5 UK average, (2) rounding differences in intermediate steps, (3) some suppliers use slightly different VCF values. For maximum accuracy, enter your exact CV from your gas bill into the calculator above.
Does this calculator work for both metric and imperial meters?
This specific calculator is designed for metric meters (which show readings in m³). If you have an imperial meter (which shows readings in ft³ or cubic feet), please use our Cubic Feet to kWh Calculator or the Gas Units to kWh Calculator which handles both meter types.
